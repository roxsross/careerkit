# Professional portrait prompt

**Intended use:** [LinkedIn / CV / speaker bio]
**Style:** [name]

## Main prompt
> [Explicitly use the supplied reference photo. Preserve identifiable facial features, skin tone, natural texture, apparent age and hair identity; describe light, background, composition, clothing, crop and expression.]

## Avoid
> Identity changes, synthetic plastic skin, altered facial geometry, overly dramatic filters, text/watermarks, distorted hands or eyes.

## Review before use
- Is the person still recognizable?
- Is the face clear in a small circular crop?
- Is this an accurate and appropriate representation for the intended context?
